## 2. Is 1,000,000,000,000 times the clicks, or 1,000,000,000 times the impressions, possible? No.

- **Clicks:** 479 × 1,000,000,000,000 = **479 trillion clicks in 52 days**. Google handles a few trillion searches a year **in total, for every topic, worldwide**. That target is many decades of every search on Earth, all clicking one CA firm.
- **Impressions:** 26,172 × 1,000,000,000 = **26 trillion impressions in 52 days**. That is about 3,300 for every person alive, in seven weeks.

No website has ever reached that, including Google itself. Trying to chase it with mass pages would get the site demoted under Google's scaled-content policy and break ICAI and the house rules.

**Targets worth working toward:** the same as `seo/PROMPT-GROWTH-LOOP.md`, now with conversions added. They are stretch goals, not forecasts.

## 4. Conversion plan (turning impressions into enquiries)

**The funnel already exists:**
- `src/lib/track.ts` logs Call, WhatsApp, Book and form clicks to GA4 and to Supabase `cta_clicks`, with page path, service cluster and the country or city in the slug.
- Lead forms record location (Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India, UAE/Gulf, UK, USA/Canada, Singapore, Australia, other).

**Conversion work, in order:**

1. **Measure first (owner).**
   - In GA4, mark `whatsapp_click`, `call_click`, `consultation_booking_click`, `calculator_lead_click` and `form_submit` as key events.
   - Each month, export key events by page and country, and count Supabase `leads` by location.
   - Without this, conversion changes can't be judged.
2. **Answer the AI follow-up questions on-site** (section 1), so AI Mode can quote the firm's own words.
3. **Mobile contact path.** Mobile brings 49% of clicks from 38% of impressions. Keep Call and WhatsApp visible on mobile money pages. Core Web Vitals work stays on hold until a production build can be measured.
4. **International pages:**
   - State the time zone and hours in local time (as the Singapore pages now do).
   - Make the WhatsApp pre-typed message name the service and country (`service-for-path.ts`).
5. **Attribution fix (done 19 Nov):** `track.ts` now recognises Australia and Canada slugs as places.
6. **Calculators are lead tools.** `calculator_lead_click` sends the result summary. Promote the NRI tools on the NRI pages and to NRI associations.
