# Service-by-service plan: enquiries from search, October 2026

Prepared 16 October 2026 for C S Rushil & Co. Built from the repository and the live site. This plan sets targets and lists what each one depends on. It doesn't promise a ranking position, a fixed number of calls or a daily client count. Google decides rankings, and client numbers depend on the firm's own follow-up and capacity.

## 1. The honest numbers

- 100 enquiries a day is about 3,000 a month. The nine services below, at 1 to 2 a day each, give 9 to 18 a day. That is the realistic target for this list, reached over 6 to 12 months. The rest of the way needs more services, more cities, paid channels and more of the firm's capacity.
- A chat rate of 3% of visitors (the planning assumption in the roadmap) means about 300 visits a day from these pages for 9 chats. Measure the real rate before trusting it.
- Ranking "on top" for every search is not something any firm can guarantee. Search results change daily, and rankings depend on competitors, reviews, local proximity and links.

## 3. What each page needs for answer engines (AEO)

Apply to all nine pages, one at a time, checked against the page:

1. A direct answer of 40 to 60 words, directly under the first question-style heading, that states the answer in the first sentence.
2. Six to eight FAQs, each answer 40 to 60 words, matching the visible text exactly.
3. One comparison or timeline table, where it helps the reader.
4. Numbered steps where the process has steps (the how-to block already exists on all nine).
5. One official citation to the MCA, GST portal or CBIC, cited in the text, not in a footer.
6. No fee figures. The fee table waits for the firm's own figures.

**Months 3 to 6**

## 7. Rules that apply to every step

- No fee or price claims on any page, post, profile or ad.
- No promise of outcomes, ranking positions or guaranteed client numbers.
- No comparison with other firms; no review solicitation beyond existing clients after completed work.
- Enquiries go to WhatsApp only. Nothing is stored on the website.
- Every addition is checked against the firm's facts. Anything unverified is marked "to confirm" for CA Rushil.

## 8. What the firm must provide or decide

1. The ICAI membership number and FRN.
2. Confirmation of the working hours and the meeting options for clients outside Chennai.
3. The 2026 government and professional fee schedule, if a fee table is wanted (the firm supplies the figures; none is written here).
4. Whether to run search ads for these services (check ICAI advertising guidelines first).
5. A decision on which of the four "to confirm" items in `seo/CA-SIGNOFF.md` the firm will sign off.

## 9. What this plan does not cover

- Rankings in other countries, and any tax advice for those countries.
- Live rendering checks on mobile (a person must check in a browser).
- Google Business Profile reviews already received (these need the profile's own data).
