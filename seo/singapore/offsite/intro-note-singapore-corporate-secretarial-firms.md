Subject: Indian-side filings for your clients with Indian subsidiaries

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am CA Rushil C S of C S Rushil & Co., Chartered Accountants, with our one office in Anna Nagar, Chennai. We work in English and Tamil and serve Singapore clients online.

Some of your clients will own, or plan to set up, an Indian subsidiary. You handle their ACRA and IRAS work. We handle the Indian side: FEMA reporting such as FC-GPR and the FLA return, ROC filings, Indian income tax and TDS, and the transfer pricing report. We do not advise on Singapore tax, ACRA, IRAS or Singapore company law.

I would like to propose a simple referral relationship. When a client of yours needs Indian filings, you could introduce us. When a client of ours needs Singapore work, we would point them to you. In both directions, the client chooses its adviser.

To be clear, there is no fee-sharing and no commission, either way. Neither firm pays the other for any introduction, and neither of us would charge a client for it.

If it is useful, we could also write a short factual guide on Indian-side filings together, for your clients to read.

If you would like to talk, a reply to this note is enough.

With regards,
CA Rushil C S
C S Rushil & Co., Chartered Accountants
Anna Nagar, Chennai
