## Recommended wording constraints

- May say: a foreign holding company merging into its Indian wholly owned subsidiary is a class named in rule 25(1A)(vi) of the 2016 Rules (as amended 4 Sep 2025), which points to the fast-track route; may not say it is automatic, or list its full conditions, until rule 25A(5) is read.
- May say: the fast-track steps are notice (CAA-9, 30 days for objections), solvency declaration (CAA-10) and filing with the Central Government (CAA-11, within 15 days after meetings). Do not name the Regional Director's confirmation.
- May say: the Cross Border Merger Regulations treat compliant transactions as deemed RBI approved, give two years for foreign borrowings and non-permitted assets, and require reports as RBI prescribes. May not say 180 days, name a reporting form, or name the AD bank route for the merger itself.
- May say: shares issued to non-residents after the merger are reported on FC-GPR within 30 days of issue. May not name the Single Master Form or FC-TRS.
- Tell readers to re-check the 2026 RBI amendment before saying who approves the scheme (NCLT versus "Competent Authority"); the RBI page shows "Competent Authority" replacing "NCLT" in places from 5 Jun 2026.
- May say: resident shareholders' exit on merger or liquidation of the foreign entity needs competent authority approval under the Overseas Investment Rules (rule 17(3)); an employee's ESOP in the overseas entity is permitted under Schedule III para 3 if offered globally on a uniform basis. May not say ESOPs are replaced by Indian ESOPs after a merger.
- Tax: do not cite 2(1B), 47(vi), 47(vii), 72A, 79, the 2025 Act section numbers, the three-fourths test, or whether a foreign company qualifies as an amalgamating company until the Act text is read from incometaxindia.gov.in or India Code. Write "tax neutrality depends on conditions; get tax advice" and nothing more specific.
- Do not quote a duration or cost for NCLT or fast-track mergers; no official average was found.
- Case studies: Flipkart (completed, Reuters, 9 Mar 2026), Pine Labs (final NCLT approval Apr 2025; DRHP reported Jul 2025), Zepto (completed, PTI, 28 Jan 2025), Udaan (proposed, Entrackr 18 Mar 2026). Describe each as "reported", with the date; do not state tax paid or structure details beyond what is above.
- Press Note 3, ss.233 and 234 text, INC-28 and MGT-14 timelines: mention only as "check" items with no figures until read. Singapore side: one sentence, "your Singapore counsel".
