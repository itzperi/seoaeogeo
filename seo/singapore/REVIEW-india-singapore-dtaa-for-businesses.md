The Income-tax Act, 2025 and 1961 Act sections and the Income-tax Rules, 2026 were read on incometaxindia.gov.in; the Department's server refuses automated requests on the www host, so the pages were read through the Department's own mirror host (wm.incometaxindia.gov.in) using the same page slugs, and the URLs in the table are the canonical www addresses. Where a PDF or page could not be read, the claim is marked NOT CONFIRMED and the table says what is needed. Result key: CONFIRMED = matches the primary source; CORRECTED = the guide differs from the source and a fix is proposed in "Discrepancies found"; NOT CONFIRMED = could not be verified against a primary source.

## Discrepancies found

### CORRECTED

**Rows 55 and 56 (H2 3 para 1 and Table 1, column "Documents needed")**

- Guide text, H2 3 para 1: "Four: a Certificate of Residence (COR) from the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) covering the period of payment; Form 41, the online declaration that replaced Form 10F under the Income-tax Rules, 2026; a beneficial ownership declaration; and, for royalties and service fees, a declaration that the Singapore company has no permanent establishment in India."
- Guide text, Table 1 header: "Documents needed", with COR, Form 41, beneficial ownership declaration and (for royalties and fees) no-PE declarations in each row.
- What the primary source says: section 159(8) of the Income-tax Act, 2025 requires only (a) a certificate of residence from the government of the non-resident's country and (b) "such other documents and information, as may be prescribed". The prescribed information is Form 41 (Rule 75; user manual panels: Part A applicant, Part B residential information, declaration). Neither a beneficial ownership declaration nor a no-PE declaration is a statutory or prescribed document; they are what Indian payers commonly ask for.
- Proposed corrected wording, H2 3 para 1: "Section 159(8) requires two things: a Certificate of Residence (COR) from the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) covering the period of payment, and the information prescribed in Form 41, the online form that replaced Form 10F under the Income-tax Rules, 2026. Indian payers also usually ask for a beneficial ownership declaration and, for royalties and service fees, a declaration that the Singapore company has no permanent establishment in India. These two are the payer's own records, not prescribed forms."
- Proposed corrected wording, Table 1: change the column header to "Documents to collect" and in each row write, for example, "Required by section 159(8): COR and Form 41. Payer's records: beneficial ownership declaration; share register for the 10% rate". For royalties and fees add "no-PE declaration; licence or service agreement" under payer's records. Optionally change the H2 to "Which documents should a Singapore company have to claim the treaty rate?"

**Row 83 (H2 5, Hareon paragraph)**
