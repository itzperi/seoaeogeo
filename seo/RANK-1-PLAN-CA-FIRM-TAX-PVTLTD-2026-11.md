## 5. Map pack plan (the biggest lever for "CA firm" and "tax consultant")

1. **Reviews, steadily, from real clients.**
   - Target: 57 → 150 in 6 months (about 4 a week), then keep going.
   - Ask every client at the end of each completed engagement: a filed return, an incorporated company, a closed audit. Send the direct review link (`GBP_REVIEW_URL` in `src/lib/site.ts`).
   - Never offer anything in return, never script the wording, never post reviews in bulk. Google filters all three.
   - Reply to every review within 48 hours.
2. **Categories.**
   - Primary: *Chartered accountant*.
   - Secondary: *Tax consultant*, *Tax preparation service*, *Accounting firm*, *Auditor*, *Accountant*.
   - Without *Tax consultant* as a category, the profile is a weak match for that search.
3. **Services.** List every service from `seo/GBP-SERVICES-AND-PROFILE-2026-11-08.md`, including "Private limited company registration", "Income tax return filing", "Tax consultant" and "NRI tax".
4. **Description.** Open with "Chartered accountancy firm (CA firm) in Anna Nagar, Chennai" and name tax, GST, audit and company incorporation. No superlatives or fees (ICAI).
5. **Weekly posts and photos.** Office, team and signage photos, plus one post a week on a deadline: advance tax 15 December, GSTR-9 31 December, ROC dates. The ROC calendar now on `/roc-compliances` supplies months of material.
6. **Q&A.** Add the common questions with factual answers (hours, walk-in, online service, languages).
7. **Track by area, not from one spot.** Check the map pack from Anna Nagar, Kilpauk, Nungambakkam, T Nagar, Adyar and OMR every fortnight. A geo-grid tool such as Local Falcon, or the seo-maps skill with DataForSEO, does this.
