## 2. The plan, by lever (in order of impact)

### Lever 1: reviews (prominence). The single biggest factor.

| Milestone | Reviews | Pace | Effect expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| Now | 57 | ~0 a month recently | #8–12 at the office |
| +6 weeks | 80 | 4 a week | Top 3 at the office point |
| +3 months | 110–120 | 15–20 a month | #1 within about 2 km; top 3 to about 3–4 km |
| +6 months | 150–170 | 15 a month | Top 3 in Kilpauk, Mogappair, Shenoy Nagar |
| +12 months | 250+ | 12–15 a month | Competing in central Chennai. S. Madhavan's 726 is out of reach in 12 months; the rest of the pack is not |

**How:**
- **When to ask:** after every completed piece of work (filed return, incorporation, closed notice, audit), in person or by WhatsApp.
- **Link:** the short link **https://www.csrushil.com/review**, which already redirects to the Google review form.
- **QR code:** print Google's own code from the profile dashboard (Home → **Ask for reviews** → QR code) at reception and on the engagement-close email.
- **Message** (CA Rushil to confirm against ICAI guidance first): "If our work on your [service] was useful, a Google review in your own words helps other businesses find us. Thank you."
- **Assign one owner** (a team member) and a weekly count, reviewed on Monday.
- **Rules:**
  - no review text written for clients (one review already has pasted template text: "[Income tax filing/GST compliance]");
  - no incentives, and no asking only happy clients;
  - no office tablet or Wi-Fi (same-IP reviews get filtered);
  - no bursts.
- **Replies:** reply to every review within 48 hours. Thank the reviewer without naming their business or the work.
- **Wording that helps relevance:** when clients mention what you did in their own words ("company registration", "GST notice", "audit", "Anna Nagar"), Google reads those words as relevance. Ask them to say what the work was; never script it.

### Lever 2: competitors' keyword-stuffed names (relevance; fast and legitimate)
Google's guidelines say the business name must be the real-world name. If "Rits & Associates | Chartered Accountants in Anna Nagar" or "K.Balamurugan CA, Chartered Accountants in Chennai" is not the name on their signage and registration:
1. On Google Maps, open the listing → **Suggest an edit** → **Change name or other details** → enter the real name, with a short reason.
2. If that's not accepted, use Google's **Business Redressal Complaint Form** with evidence: a photo of the signage, or the ICAI firm name from the public register.
3. Report the keyword-only listing "PRIVATE LIMITED COMPANY REGISTRATION" the same way.

When Google corrects a stuffed name, that listing usually drops, and the profiles behind it move up. **Never add keywords to C S Rushil's own name:** it risks suspension.
