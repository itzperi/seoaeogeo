## On targets ("100-1,000 calls/month")

I'm not going to hand you a number here, and you should be skeptical of
anyone who does. Call volume from local SEO depends on: search volume for
these terms in Chennai (which I don't have live data for in this session —
DataForSEO or Google Keyword Planner would give real numbers), your GBP
completeness and review count relative to the named competitors in
`COMPETITOR-ANALYSIS.md`, your actual conversion rate from profile
view → call, and time (local SEO compounds over months, not days). What I
can commit to: every on-site lever in Phases 2–3 is now pulled. The
realistic next constraint is Phase 1 (GBP), which is entirely in your hands.
