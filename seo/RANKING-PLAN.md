# Ranking Plan — 4 Target Keywords + Full Keyword Expansion

## First, the mechanism you need to know before anything else

You asked: *"if anyone searches from any part of Chennai we should be
ranking first."* Whether that's achievable depends entirely on which **type**
of search result the keyword triggers — this isn't a website limitation,
it's how Google's ranking systems are built.

### Type A: Organic/content results — city-wide #1 is a real, achievable goal

**Private Limited Company Incorporation** and **GST Litigation** trigger
standard organic results. These are not weighted by the searcher's exact
location within Chennai — a well-optimised page can rank #1 for "GST
litigation Chennai" for someone searching from Anna Nagar, Tambaram, or
Sholinganallur equally. This is squarely a content/SEO problem, and it's the
one this rebuild has been built to win.

### Type B: Local Pack results — proximity to the searcher is a ranking factor Google itself controls

**Chartered Accountant near me** and **Best CA nearby** trigger the Local
Pack (the map + 3 listings block). Google's local ranking algorithm
explicitly weights **distance between the searcher and the business** as one
of three core factors, alongside relevance and prominence. This means:

- Someone searching from **Anna Nagar or nearby** (Ambattur, Kilpauk, Aminjikarai,
  Villivakkam) will realistically see you in or near the local pack once your
  GBP is optimised.
- Someone searching from **Tambaram, Sholinganallur, or Porur** (15-25km away)
  will very likely see a CA firm physically closer to them instead — **no
  amount of website or GBP work overrides physical distance**. This is true
  for every business on Google, not a gap specific to your site.

**What this means practically:** the honest, achievable version of your goal
is: **#1 organic ranking for Private Limited Company Incorporation and GST
Litigation, city-wide** (Type A — already built, see below), plus **strong
local-pack presence for "near me"/"nearby" searches from Anna Nagar and the
surrounding 5-8km radius, and strong organic (non-map) ranking for those
same phrases city-wide** (Type B's organic component isn't distance-limited
the same way the map pack is). If you want map-pack presence across all of
Chennai specifically, the only mechanisms that actually do that are opening
additional verified physical locations, or running Local Service Ads / Google
Ads — not organic SEO.

   itself) over building doorway pages — this is what Google's own
   guidelines recommend for single-location service businesses.

## Part 3: What actually moves the needle, in order

1. **Google Business Profile** (`GBP-CHECKLIST.md`) — blocks both "near me"
   keywords entirely until done. This is the single highest-leverage
   remaining task and it's 100% yours to complete.
2. **Citations on Sulekha, Justdial, ICAI, IndiaMART** (`LISTING-COPY.md`) —
   reinforces NAP consistency, which both Local Pack and organic ranking use
   as a trust signal.
3. **Reviews** — specifically decides "Best CA nearby." Start the
   request-flow now; this compounds over months, not days.
4. **Backlinks to the 2 flagship pages** — the incorporation and GST
   litigation pages need external sites linking to them specifically, not
   just to the homepage. Directory listings, ICAI profile, and any press or
   guest-post opportunities should link to the specific service URL where
   relevant.
5. **Publish the blog content calendar on schedule** — topical authority
   compounds; a burst of 11 posts today and then silence for 6 months reads
   as less authoritative to Google than steady, consistent publishing.
