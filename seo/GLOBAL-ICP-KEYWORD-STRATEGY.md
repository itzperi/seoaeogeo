> **Method note.** No keyword-volume tool was used, so **no search volumes are stated anywhere in this document**. Difficulty labels (Low / Medium / High) are judgements from live Google SERP spot-checks run on 3 Oct 2026 (see Appendix A) plus who typically ranks for that query type. Engagement value tiers (Low / Mid / High) are relative, and no fee figures are given.
>
> **ICAI guardrails apply to every page and asset below:** no "best / top / #1 / leading / trusted by" claims, no fee-discount or "cheapest" language, no testimonials used to solicit work, no comparisons with other firms, and no guaranteed outcomes ("guaranteed refund", "win your case"). Write factual, informational copy. Before publishing, check each page against `seo/ICAI-COMPLIANCE.md`.
>
> **Statute note.** The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026. On every direct-tax, NRI, trust and TP page, cite **both** the old section (people still search "44AB", "12A", "80G", "195") **and** the new-Act reference. Verify every new section number against the bare Act before publishing. Do not guess them.

### 5.2 What "1,000 visits/day and 10 booked meetings/day" would actually require

| Metric | Target | Implication |

| Visits | 1,000/day ≈ **30,000/month** | About **400× today's 75 clicks/month**.
