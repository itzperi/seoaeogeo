## Ongoing (weekly/monthly — this is what "near me" ranking actually rewards)

- **Photos:** upload real photos of the Anna Nagar office (exterior signage,
  reception, team at work), not stock images. Google explicitly weights
  listings with more, more recent photos in local ranking.
- **GBP Posts:** post updates 1–2x/month — a new blog post, a service
  reminder (e.g. "GST return due dates this month"), a team update.
- **Reviews:** this is the single biggest lever for "Best CA nearby"
  specifically.
  - After every completed engagement, send a WhatsApp/email with a direct
    review link (Google gives you a short link from the GBP dashboard).
  - Respond to every review — positive and negative — within a few days.
    Response rate and recency are themselves ranking signals, not just
    reputation management.
  - Aim for a steady drip (a few new reviews a month) rather than a one-time
    push of 20 reviews at once, which can look unnatural to Google.
- **Q&A section:** seed it yourself with 3–5 common questions (e.g. "Do you
  handle GST notice replies?", "Is the first consultation free?") and answer
  them as the business.

## What NOT to do

- Don't stuff the business name field with keywords (e.g. "C S Rushil & Co —
  Best Chartered Accountant Chennai GST Company Registration") — this
  violates Google's guidelines and risks suspension.
- Don't create a second listing for a virtual/coworking address just to rank
  in more areas — Google actively penalizes this ("service area" businesses
  should use the service-area radius setting instead, not multiple pins).
