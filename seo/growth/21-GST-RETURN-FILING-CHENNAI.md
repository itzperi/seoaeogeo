## 1. Executive summary

**Goal.** 10 qualified enquiries a day (300 a month) for GST return filing, as one input to 100 clients a month firm-wide. A qualified enquiry needs a real need, one useful detail (GSTIN type, turnover band, number of invoices, months pending, scheme), WhatsApp/phone/form/booking contact, and likely purchase within 0 to 90 days.

**What the site has today.** A solid money page with a due-date table, a late-fee table, eight FAQs and a five-step process. Eight audience pages with distinct content. Four older blog posts of 430 to 580 words each, thin and without verify labels. No tools, no comparison content, no FAQ hub, no WhatsApp pre-filled messages per audience.

**Where the enquiries will come from.** GST return filing is a recurring service, so the buyer is usually one of four people: (1) a business owner whose accountant left or whose returns are pending (urgent, BOFU); (2) a new GSTIN holder with a first return coming (BOFU, tied to plan 20); (3) a seller or exporter who knows the scheme choice or ITC reconciliation is wrong (MOFU, high value); (4) a finance lead comparing CA-managed filing against in-house or software (MOFU). The plan puts the most enquiry weight on the money page, the five upgraded audience pages and the five problem pages, because those match "I need this filed" and "I am behind".

**Five moves, in order of impact**
1. Re-title and re-meta the money page so "GST return filing Chennai" is in the title, fix the two things that look wrong on the live page (description is 157 characters; the `StartingFees` fee component, which the ICAI rule forbids), and add a verify register, a Chennai/TN section, a 24-FAQ block and a WhatsApp-first CTA.
2. Upgrade five audience pages (ecommerce, freelancers, restaurants, import-export, manufacturers) with a monthly return checklist per audience, a WhatsApp message per audience, one Chennai-specific section each, and a link to the right tool.
3. Publish the problem cluster first (pending returns, wrong GSTR-3B, ITC not in GSTR-2B, late fee, portal errors). These are the urgent searches.
4. Ship three return-specific tools (GSTR-2B reconciliation checklist, return due-date tracker, filing scheme selector) as lead magnets.
5. Upgrade the four owned blog posts from about 500 words to 1,100 to 1,600 words with verify labels, sources and a WhatsApp CTA, and add the four new comparison posts.
