## 1. Executive summary

### 1.1 The opportunity in one paragraph

A business owner who types "GST notice reply Chennai" has an ASMT-10, DRC-01A or DRC-01 in hand and a date on it. This is the strongest enquiry-creating moment in the whole GST cluster: real need, a notice type, a deadline, and a buying window of days, not months. The page that serves this reader exists and is structurally sound, but it is a thin `ServicePageTemplate` page with six notice cards, eight FAQs and one related post. It has no notice identifier, no deadline helper, no document list by notice type, no Chennai/Tamil Nadu jurisdiction content, no way to triage "notice or litigation?" and no supporting cluster. This plan turns it into the early-stage GST notice hub, with 27 new URLs under it, and leaves everything after an order to `/gst-litigation-chennai`.

No search volumes, rankings or competitor positions were measured for this plan. Do a live SERP check and a Search Console export before sizing targets (action 10 in Section 14). The target in the brief is 10 qualified enquiries a day (300 a month) for this service. Nothing in this plan promises it; Section 12 sets the baseline-first measurement that tells you how far each page moves toward it.

4. **Upgrade two existing posts** (S1 first 48 hours, S2 ITC mismatch) before writing new ones. They already exist and are linked from other pages.
5. **Add six focused supporting posts** and five problem pages that capture distinct long-tail intents without competing with the MP head terms.
6. **Local depth without doorways.** Chennai and Tamil Nadu content as sections on the MP and on four existing Chennai area pages; no new city or neighbourhood URLs.
