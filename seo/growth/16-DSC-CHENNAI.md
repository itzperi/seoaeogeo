### 1.3 Why DSC matters more than its ticket size

DSC is a low-ticket, high-frequency entry service. Every company director, designated partner, company or LLP GST signatory, exporter and tender bidder needs one, and it expires. That makes it the cheapest way for a new client to start working with the firm, and the most natural moment to ask "what are you signing?". The plan treats every DSC enquiry as a door to a higher-value service.

### 1.4 Target and honest funnel maths

The brief's target is 10 qualified enquiries a day for each priority service. Search demand for DSC in Chennai has **not been measured**, and DSC buyers often buy from the first seller they reach. So the plan does not promise 10 DSC-only QEs a day from organic search. It builds three sources and counts all of them:

| Source | How it creates QEs | How to measure |
|---|---|---|
| Organic + AI answers | MP, 23 articles, FAQ hub, tools | GSC clicks × WhatsApp click rate × QE rate |
| GBP / Maps | "DSC near me", "digital signature Anna Nagar" | GBP calls, website clicks, chat |
| Existing-client base | Renewal reminders 60/30/7 days before expiry (T2 + WA-R1–R3) | Renewals booked; cross-sells opened |

Planning formula (re-set every 4 weeks with real data): **QE/day = (organic clicks/day × WA-click rate × QE rate) + GBP actions/day × QE rate + renewal conversations/day**. Week-4 baseline replaces all assumptions. Success is judged on **qualified enquiries and cross-sold engagements**, not on DSC volume.
