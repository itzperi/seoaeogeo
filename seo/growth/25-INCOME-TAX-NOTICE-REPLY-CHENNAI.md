### 1.2 Why this service is a high-intent play

A person with an income tax notice has a date, a portal login and a real fear. The search is urgent and local intent is mild (many want a Chennai CA they can meet), but the work is done online. The notice itself is a qualifying detail: the section, the assessment year and the reply date turn a vague enquiry into a quoted job. The plan therefore pushes every visitor to share **notice type, section, assessment year and reply date** on WhatsApp.

### 12.3 Monthly review checklist

6. Check for cannibalisation: two URLs ranking for one query.
