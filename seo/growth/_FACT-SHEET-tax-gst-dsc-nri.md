## Domain facts
GST (CGST Act, 2017; portal gst.gov.in)
- Registration application REG-01, Aadhaar authentication, ARN, certificate REG-06; clarification notice REG-03, reply REG-04 [V]
- Aggregate turnover threshold: ₹40 lakh for goods and ₹20 lakh for services in Tamil Nadu. Registration is compulsory regardless of turnover for, among others, inter-state taxable supply of goods and sellers through e-commerce operators (s.24) [V]
- Returns: GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, QRMP scheme with IFF, GSTR-9 annual, GSTR-9C reconciliation above a turnover limit, composition CMP-08 and GSTR-4 [V for every due date and limit]
- Credit is based on GSTR-2B. Time limit for ITC is s.16(4); relief in s.16(5) and s.16(6)
- Notices: ASMT-10 → ASMT-11; DRC-01A (Part B objection); DRC-01 → DRC-06; ADT-01 audit; RFD-08 → RFD-09; REG-17 → REG-18 within 7 working days; revocation REG-21. Demand sections: s.73/74 for FY 2023-24 and earlier; s.74A from FY 2024-25 (42 months from the annual return due date)
- Tamil Nadu taxpayers are administered either by CBIC Central GST commissionerates or by the TN Commercial Taxes Department
- LUT for zero-rated exports: RFD-11 [V]
- Rate rationalisation in September 2025 (affects classification disputes) [V]

Income tax
- The site frames income tax under the Income-tax Act, 2025, effective 1 April 2026, replacing the 1961 Act ("tax year" concept). The site already uses new form numbers, for example TDS statements in Forms 138/140/144 (earlier 24Q/26Q/27Q) and 15CA/15CB now Forms 145/146. Every section and form number under the 2025 Act, and any mapping from 1961 numbers, is [V].
- Portal: incometax.gov.in e-filing; AIS/TIS and Form 26AS for pre-filled data; e-Proceedings for notice responses; faceless assessment [V]
- Common notice types, in 1961 Act numbering widely searched: s.143(1) intimation, s.139(9) defective return, s.143(2) scrutiny, s.148/148A reassessment, s.245 adjustment of refund. People still search with these numbers, so use them as keywords, with the new-Act mapping [V]
- ITR due dates (non-audit individuals; audit cases), belated and revised returns, ITR-U updated return: all [V]
- Old vs new regime: the new regime is the default [V]

DSC
- Issued under the Information Technology Act, 2000 by Certifying Authorities licensed by the Controller of Certifying Authorities (CCA). Class 3 is used for MCA, GST, income tax, DGFT and e-tenders. eKYC by Aadhaar, PAN-based or video verification; USB token; validity 1–3 years. Prices are set by the CA or its agent, not the government. [V] for validity options and rules.
- Required: directors and designated partners for MCA forms; companies and LLPs must sign GST and income-tax filings with DSC; audit reports [V]

NRI
- Residency test (182 days, or 60 days plus 365 days in the preceding four years with exceptions; deemed residency for certain Indian citizens; RNOR) [V], especially under the 2025 Act
- DTAA relief needs a Tax Residency Certificate and Form 10F; NRE vs NRO accounts; Schedule FA for residents with foreign assets [V]
- Buyers of property from an NRI deduct TDS; a lower or nil deduction certificate is available from the department [V]
