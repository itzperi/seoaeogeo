| Type | What it looks like at C S Rushil & Co. | Why it is high-ticket | Who sets the fee |

**HTQE (high-ticket qualified enquiry)** = the brief's qualified enquiry (real need + one useful detail + contact + likely to buy in 0 to 90 days) **plus at least one HT marker**: (a) recurring scope (monthly or annual), (b) more than one entity, filing type or year, (c) a dispute or notice with a stated amount or a hearing date, (d) a foreign parent, foreign income or foreign remittance, (e) a funded or audited company. The firm decides which enquiries count; section 8 gives a scoring rubric.

### 1.4 Funnel arithmetic (every rate is a scenario to test; nothing here is the firm's measured rate)

Chain: sessions and other sources, then enquiries, then HTQEs, then scoping calls, then proposals, then signed engagements.

| Symbol | Meaning | Value |
|---|---|---|
| T | Signed HT engagements wanted per month | **FIRM-FILL** |
| Nc | "1 in Nc scoping calls becomes a signed engagement" | Scenario: test 3 and 5. Replace with the firm's own history |
| Nq | "1 in Nq HTQEs books a scoping call" | Scenario: 2. Replace with data |
| V | Average annual engagement value | **FIRM-FILL** (not used below) |
| D | Average engagement duration as tracked by the firm | **FIRM-FILL** |

Arithmetic on the scenarios (calls needed = T × Nc; HTQEs needed = calls × Nq):

| T (signed per month) | Nc = 3: calls | Nc = 3: HTQEs | Nc = 5: calls | Nc = 5: HTQEs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 6 | 12 | 10 | 20 |
| 4 | 12 | 24 | 20 | 40 |
| 6 | 18 | 36 | 30 | 60 |

Reading it: to sign four engagements a month under the harsher scenario the firm needs about 40 HTQEs a month, about two a working day. Sessions needed = HTQEs ÷ the session-to-HTQE rate, which is unknown until GA4 and the enquiry log have run for four weeks. As a sizing illustration only, if 1 in 100 sessions on HT pages produced an HTQE (a scenario, not an estimate), 40 HTQEs would need 4,000 qualified sessions a month on those pages, against a baseline of about 75 organic clicks a month for the whole site. The gap is why the plan stacks LinkedIn, referrals, speaking and WhatsApp re-engagement on top of SEO.

### 1.5 An honest note on "10 qualified enquiries a day for every service"

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Volume | 10 a day × 30 days = 300 a month per service. For the 10 flagship offers that is 3,000 HTQEs a month |
| Sales capacity | At the scenario Nq = 2, 3,000 HTQEs means about 1,500 scoping calls a month, about 58 a working day on a 26-day month (Mon to Sat). The earlier ICP document estimates a partner can handle about 2 to 4 discovery calls a day (that document's estimate; confirm with the firm) |
| Delivery capacity | The team is 16. A signed HT engagement is months or years of work; 10 a day cannot be delivered |
| Demand | Foreign-parent entry, transfer pricing and audit are niche markets. The number of Indian subsidiaries looking for a new adviser in any month cannot be measured from this repository |
| Recommendation | Keep 10 a day as the per-service target for **domestic commodity services** (registration, GST registration, ITR) in the per-service plans. For HT offers use a **weekly HTQE target set by the firm** (the earlier ICP document suggested 3 to 5 qualified high-value conversations a week within 12 months; treat that as a starting point), tracked in section 13. Measure 10 a day only as a total across all services |
