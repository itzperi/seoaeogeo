## Verified facts you may state
- Companies Act, 2013. Private limited: min 2 directors, 2 shareholders (can be same), max 200 members, max 15 directors without special resolution; no minimum paid-up capital (Companies (Amendment) Act, 2015).
- Resident director: Section 149(3) (wording of "financial year" vs "previous calendar year" — mark verify).
- SPICe+ (INC-32) Part A name / Part B incorporation on MCA V3; e-MoA INC-33, e-AoA INC-34, AGILE-PRO-S INC-35 (PAN, TAN, GSTIN, EPFO, ESIC, bank account). Name reservation via Part A: ₹1,000; held 20 days.
- MCA filing fee zero for authorised capital up to ₹15 lakh. PAN ₹66, TAN ₹65. GSTIN/EPFO/ESIC via AGILE-PRO-S: no fee.
- Tamil Nadu stamp duty (Indian Stamp (TN Amendment) Act 2023, as published): MoA ₹200; AoA ₹500 per ₹10 lakh authorised capital or part, cap ₹5 lakh. So ₹700 at ≤₹10 lakh, ₹1,200 at ₹15 lakh, ₹5,200 at ₹1 crore. SPICe+ computes the actual figure (mark verify).
- DSC: Class 3 from licensed Certifying Authority, market-priced (not MCA fee).
- Typical time (firm's documented experience): 7–12 working days once documents and DSCs ready; ~3 weeks from scratch. LLP 10–15 working days.
- ROC Chennai at Shastri Bhavan, 26 Haddows Road, Nungambakkam (PIN unconfirmed — omit); covers Chennai and most of TN; ROC Coimbatore covers nine western districts. SPICe+ is examined by MCA Central Registration Centre (CRC).
- Common resubmission causes (firm's experience): office proof (TANGEDCO bill / GCC property tax receipt) in a name other than the NOC signatory; PAN–Aadhaar mismatch; generic main objects; name close to an existing company/trademark.
- Post-incorporation: first board meeting within 30 days (s.173(1)); first auditor by board within 30 days (s.139(6)); share certificates within 60 days (s.56(4)(a)); INC-20A within 180 days (s.10A) — penalty ₹50,000 company, ₹1,000/day per officer up to ₹1 lakh; AOC-4 / MGT-7A after AGM with ₹100/day additional fee (s.403); first AGM within 9 months of first FY end (s.96). FC-GPR on RBI FIRMS within 30 days of allotment to a foreign investor.
- NRI/foreign: passport for foreign nationals; documents apostilled (Hague countries) or consularised; FDI sector rules apply.
- LLP: LLP Act 2008, FiLLiP, Form 3 LLP agreement within 30 days, annual Form 8 and Form 11. OPC: single member, nominee consent INC-3.
