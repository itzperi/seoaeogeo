# Content Calendar — First 6 Months

The original audit flagged the blog (1 post, July 2025) as the single
biggest topical-authority gap — "High effort, High impact." This calendar
gives it a realistic cadence: 2 posts/month, each written to support one of
the four target keywords or a genuine adjacent long-tail term, not generic
filler.

## Cadence: 2 posts/month, ~1,000–1,500 words each

### Month 1
1. **"How to Choose a Chartered Accountant in Chennai"** (published at
   `/blog/how-to-choose-a-chartered-accountant-in-chennai`) — targets "best
   CA nearby" search intent with genuine buyer guidance and comparison
   criteria, without using "best" as a self-promotional superlative
   (ICAI's Code of Ethics restricts CA firms from marketing themselves with
   superlative claims). Strong AEO/GEO citation candidate.
2. **"GST Show-Cause Notice: What to Do in the First 48 Hours"** — supports
   `/gst-litigation-chennai`, highly specific and actionable (AEO-friendly
   direct-answer format).

### Month 2
3. **"SPICe+ vs. the Old MCA Incorporation Process: What Changed"** —
   supports the incorporation page with fresher, more specific content than
   competitors' generic explainers.
4. **"Private Limited vs. LLP for Chennai Startups: A 2026 Comparison"** —
   long-tail, feeds the entity-comparison table already on the incorporation
   page.

### Month 3
5. **"GST Appellate Tribunal (GSTAT): What Chennai Businesses Need to Know"**
   — GSTAT benches are newly operational in several states; genuinely
   time-sensitive, citable content for AI answer engines.
6. **"TDS Return Filing Deadlines for FY 2026-27"** — a factual/dated
   reference post; strong candidate for featured snippets and yearly
   recurring search traffic (update annually rather than rewriting).

### Month 4
7. **"MSME (Udyam) Registration After Incorporation: A Founder's Checklist"**
   — post-incorporation compliance, links back to the incorporation page.
8. **"Virtual CFO vs. Hiring a Full-Time CFO: A Cost Comparison for Chennai
   SMEs"** — supports Virtual CFO service page, comparison-table format.

### Month 5
9. **"RERA Compliance Calendar for Tamil Nadu Developers"** — supports the
   RERA page with a genuinely dated, citable reference asset.
10. **"12A and 80G Registration: A Step-by-Step Guide for Chennai NGOs"** —
    supports Trust & NGO page.

### Month 6
11. **"Statutory Audit Checklist: What Auditors Actually Ask For"** —
    supports Audit & Assurance page.
12. **Case study / client story** (once you have at least one client willing
    to be named) — this is the single highest-value E-E-A-T content type
    missing from the site per the audit, and something I cannot fabricate on
    your behalf. Even one real, named, specific story beats ten generic
    posts for both Google and AI-answer-engine trust signals.

## Format rules for every post (AEO/GEO consistency)

- Open with a direct, quotable 40–60 word answer to the title question.
- Use question-phrased H2s where natural ("How long does X take?", not just
  "Timeline").
- Include at least one list or comparison table per post.
- Cite a government source where relevant (GST portal, MCA, ICAI, CBIC) —
  the original audit flagged the total absence of outbound authority
  citations as a GEO weakness.
- Add FAQ schema to every post using the same `FAQSection` component already
  built for the service pages.
