# Weekly KPI dashboard: enquiries that matter

Review every Monday, for the previous week. Record the numbers in a shared sheet, not in this repository. Each metric names where its number comes from. Do not record client names, phone numbers or message text.

| Metric | Source | Week 1 target | Month 3 target | Month 6 target | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp chats started | WhatsApp Business chat list (count new chats) | Baseline | 5 to 10 a day | 15 to 30 a day | Planning target, not a guarantee |
| Chats by service label | WhatsApp Business labels | Baseline | Each service has chats | Priority services lead | Labels: Income tax, GST, Company, Audit, NRI, Foreign company, Virtual CFO |
| Meetings booked | Calendly (ceo-csrushil/30min) | Baseline | 30 in the first 90 days | Rising | Count bookings, not clicks |
| Meetings held | Calendly and firm diary | Baseline | Matches bookings | Matches bookings | Track no-shows |
| Retainers signed | Firm records | 0 | Track each one | Track each one | Compliance, subsidiary CFO and transfer pricing matter most |
| GBP calls | Google Business Profile insights | Baseline | Rising | Rising | Shown in the profile insights |
| GBP direction requests | Google Business Profile insights | Baseline | Rising | Rising | Anna Nagar office |
| GBP website clicks | Google Business Profile insights | Baseline | Rising | Rising | Should point to the matching service page |
| Search Console clicks, target queries | Search Console (performance, query filter) | Baseline | Rising | Rising | Queries: income tax consultant Chennai, GST litigation Chennai, private limited company registration Chennai |
| Search Console clicks by country | Search Console (country filter) | Baseline | Track US, UK, UAE, Singapore | Track each country | Shows whether country pages earn clicks |
| Reviews received | Google Business Profile | Baseline | 2 to 3 a week from requests | Steady | Only from requests to existing clients after completed work |
| Review rating | Google Business Profile | Baseline | Keep at or above baseline | Keep at or above baseline | Report, do not act on single reviews |

## Weekly routine (10 minutes)

1. Count new WhatsApp chats and the labels on them.
2. Check the Calendly bookings for the week.
3. Note any retainer signed.
4. Read the GBP insights and the Search Console summary.
5. Reply to any new review within 48 hours (see the GBP pack, section 8).
6. Write one line on what changed and one action for next week.

## Monthly review

- Compare each service's chats with its meetings and retainers. Put more effort behind the services that convert.
- Check which country pages earn clicks. Upgrade the weakest one only after checking the content.
- Confirm that no form, lead store or click log has been added (run `python scripts/seo-check.py` and the grep in the execution report).

## Limits of this dashboard

- WhatsApp chat counts show interest, not qualified work. Track meetings and retainers alongside them.
- Search Console and GBP do not show visitor-level data, by design.
- The month 3 and month 6 targets are planning numbers, not forecasts.
