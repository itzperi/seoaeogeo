## 2. What "first page" means, and the targets

Google shows a different map pack to every searcher, depending on where they are. Nobody ranks #1 for "CA firm in Chennai" across the whole city, not even S. Madhavan with 726 reviews. Targets are therefore set by distance from the office:

| Ring | Areas | Today | Week 6 | Week 12 | Week 24 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Up to 2 km | Anna Nagar, Thirumangalam, Shenoy Nagar | 5–12 | **Top 3** | Top 3 | #1–2 |
| 2–5 km | Kilpauk, Mogappair, Aminjikarai, Arumbakkam, Villivakkam | Not ranked | Top 20 | **Top 10** | Top 3 |
| 5–10 km | Nungambakkam, Kodambakkam, Ambattur, Kolathur, T Nagar | 38–55 or not ranked | Top 40 | **Top 20** (local page 1) | Top 10 |

"First page" is met when the profile is in the 3-pack for searchers in ring 1, and in the top 20 of the local list for most of ring 2 and parts of ring 3.

## 4. Clean up competitors' keyword-stuffed names (owner, 1 hour, legitimate)
*Frameworks: local-seo-audit, the competitor section of localseodata-tool.*

Google's guidelines do not allow keywords in a business name unless they are part of the real-world name. These profiles rank above us partly because of the extra words:

| Profile | Name on Google | Likely real name |
|---|---|---|
| K.Balamurugan CA (#1 Anna Nagar) | "K.Balamurugan CA, Chartered Accountants in Chennai" | K.Balamurugan CA |
| Rits & Associates (#3 Anna Nagar) | "Rits & Associates \| Chartered Accountants in Anna Nagar" | Rits & Associates |
| G R Kumar & Co LLP (#9 citywide) | "G R Kumar & Co LLP. Top best ca firm Chennai, Gst, income tax, internal audit..." | G R Kumar & Co LLP |
| BBig Consultants | "BBig Consultants \| Best Auditing and Tax Chartered Accountants in Chennai" | BBig Consultants |

**Steps:**
1. On each profile, use **Suggest an edit**, then Name, and enter the real name. Do it from a personal account, one profile a day.
2. If the edit does not stick within 2 weeks, file the **Google Business Redressal Complaint Form**. Attach evidence of the real name: a shopfront photo, the firm name on the ICAI firm register, the website header.
3. Log each submission in `seo/progress/`.

**Expected effect:** if Google corrects even two of the Anna Nagar names, we move up one or two places in ring 1 with no other change.

Report only names that break the rule. Never make false reports, and never report a competitor's reviews to harm them.

## 5. Prominence: reviews, citations, links and brand searches

### 5a. Reviews (owner and staff, ongoing)
*Frameworks: whitespark-tool (Reputation Builder workflow, done manually), local-seo-audit.*

**Targets:**
- 4 to 5 new reviews a week, steadily, rather than in bursts.
- 80 reviews by week 6, 110 by week 12, 160 by week 24.
- Keep the rating at 4.8 or higher.

**Why:**
- In citywide results, review count works as a tiebreaker on prominence.
- The citywide #1 has 65 reviews, and the #2 has 97.
- Overtaking S. Madhavan (726) is not needed for the targets above.

**Process:**
1. After every completed engagement, the CA or staff member who did the work asks in person.
2. On the same day, send the WhatsApp short link `g.page/r/<id>/review` with one line: "If our work helped, a short Google review describing the service you used would help others find us."
3. Clients naturally name the service ("GST registration", "company incorporation"). Do not script their words.

**Rules (ICAI and Google):**
- No incentives or discounts for reviews.
- No review stations in the office.
- Staff and family do not review.
- Do not ask only happy clients ("review gating").

**Replies:**
- Reply to every review within 48 hours, in 2 to 3 sentences.
- Mention the service and "Anna Nagar" where it is natural.
- Never confirm client details in a reply.

## 9. What not to do
- No fake reviews, review swaps, or reviews from staff or family.
- No keyword stuffing in our own business name, even though rivals do it.
- No second profile, virtual office or "branch" listing.
- No prices, discounts or superlatives ("best", "top", "No.1") in the profile, posts or website. These are ICAI and brief rules.
- No paid or spam links.
- Do not change the primary category away from Chartered accountant.
- Do not change the address format or phone number anywhere unless it changes on the profile first.
