# Google Business Profile content pack: winning topics (Nov 2026 to Feb 2027)

**Purpose:** keep the profile active every week, and send profile visitors to the guides that already earn impressions. The map pack doesn't show for informational searches, but an active profile helps the local ones: "CA firms in Chennai", "chartered accountant near me", "auditor in Chennai", "tax consultant in Chennai".

**Rules (Google + ICAI):**
- No fees, "free", offers, superlatives or client names.
- Use a real office or team photo, or a plain branded card (no stock people).
- Up to 1,500 characters; these are about 600.
- Link with UTM tags so GA4 shows Maps traffic.
- Post one a week (Monday). If a date or rule changes, edit the post.

UTM pattern: `?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=<slug>`

## 5. Review requests (the biggest lever for "CA firms in Chennai")

- **When to ask:** after each completed piece of work, in person or by WhatsApp, with the link `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJzXoB0DFnUjoRv2EUuO2l7n8`.
- **Suggested wording:** "If our work on your [service] was useful, a Google review in your own words helps other businesses find us. Thank you." No templates of review text, no incentives, no asking only happy clients.
- **ICAI check:** CA Rushil confirms this wording against current ICAI guidance first.
- **Target:** 10–20 a month. Reply within 48 hours without naming the client's business or the work done.
