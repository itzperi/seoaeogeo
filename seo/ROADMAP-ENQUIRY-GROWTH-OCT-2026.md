> Date: 14 October 2026
> Scope: income tax (HNIs and families in Anna Nagar, other metros, NRIs abroad); GST litigation and registration; company incorporation; capital gains; annual compliance; Form 15CA/15CB
> Basis: the October 2026 audit (live crawl of 166 URLs, repository code, GBP field checks). No analytics or lead counts were available, so the baseline below is a labelled model to replace with your numbers.

## 1. The honest answer on "100 enquiries a day"

100 enquiries a day is about 3,000 a month. At an assumed 3% of visitors who enquire (a planning figure, not a measured rate), that needs about 100,000 visits a month. Most Chennai CA firms get a few hundred to a few thousand. So 100 a day is not a 90-day target, and no single channel delivers it.

A realistic path is staged:

| Stage | Enquiries per day | Enquiries per month | What makes it possible |
|---|---|---|---|
| Now (month 0) | Measure first | Baseline | Live site must match the repo, GBP updated, analytics connected |
| Month 3 | 5 to 10 | 150 to 300 | Local map pack, city pages indexed, reviews flowing |
| Month 6 | 15 to 30 | 450 to 900 | Content clusters ranking, GST and company pages converting, paid tests working |
| Month 12 | 40 to 70 | 1,200 to 2,100 | Strong search and AI presence, referrals, repeat clients |
| 100+ a day | Only with a proven paid channel and a larger team | not a planning assumption | Not reachable on current capacity |

The higher-value goal is not the daily count. One annual compliance or subsidiary retainer is worth more than dozens of one-off filings. Track qualified meetings and retainers, not raw enquiries.

## 2. Who to target, and how to reach them within ICAI rules

These rules shape everything below:
- No advertising that solicits work, names competitors, quotes fees or promises outcomes.
- No commission or referral fee to introducers (banks, lawyers, brokers).
- Don't target named individuals or buy lists of individuals.
- Check the current ICAI advertising guidelines before any paid ad, and keep ad copy informational.

"HNIs in Anna Nagar" can't be reached as a list of people. They are reached through the topics they search for and through local search (the map pack, the Anna Nagar and Besant Nagar pages, and tax content).

## 3. Channels in priority order

1. **Google Business Profile (highest return, start now).** Update categories, description, services and service areas, using the GBP Word file. Post weekly. Ask every satisfied client for a review. The map pack drives most local enquiries for "CA near me" and "tax consultant Anna Nagar" searches.
2. **Website pages (built; need the deploy).** The HNI pillar, six Chennai neighbourhood pages, Tamil Nadu city pages, and the GST and company pillars. These only count once the live site matches the repo.
3. **Content (two posts a month).** Topics that bring enquiries: capital gains on Chennai property, GST notice reply, incorporation steps, NRI filing, GSTAT appeals. Each post links to its service page and to contact.
4. **AI search (ongoing).** AI crawlers are allowed. Keep answers short and specific, with FAQ and speakable markup. Check that AI tools cite the firm for its key questions.
5. **WhatsApp funnel (built).** Each page's WhatsApp button pre-types the service and place, so enquiries arrive qualified. Reply within the working day and log each enquiry's source.
6. **Google Ads (test after month 3).** Small budgets on GST notice reply, company registration and income tax notice searches, sent to matching pages. Measure cost per meeting and stop anything above target.
7. **LinkedIn (founders and foreign companies).** A monthly post from CA Rushil on a practical topic, linked to the India-entry pages.
8. **Referrals (no fees).** Ask satisfied clients and other professionals to recommend the firm. No commissions.
9. **Webinars (from month 4).** Short free sessions on NRI tax, capital gains and GST notices, recorded for the video page.

## 4. Conversion: turning enquiries into meetings

- **Response time:** WhatsApp reply within two working hours, Monday to Saturday 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM. Out-of-hours auto-reply with the booking link.
- **Qualify once:** service, city, urgency and deadline. The existing form asks these.
- **Book the meeting:** the Calendly link on every page (https://calendly.com/ceo-csrushil/30min).
- **Track each enquiry in WhatsApp:** use a label for the service and city, and a status label (new, meeting booked, retainer). See section 8.
- **Follow up:** a second contact after 48 hours for every unbooked meeting.

## 6. Measurement

Track weekly:
- Enquiries by source (WhatsApp, form, call, booking), by service and by city.
- Meetings booked and held; retainers signed.
- Search Console clicks and impressions for the target queries.
- GBP calls, direction requests and website clicks.
- Review count and rating.

First 90-day target: at least 150 enquiries a month and 30 meetings. If enquiries lag, check ranking data before changing content.

## 7. Risks and what not to do

- Don't buy lead lists or target named people.
- Don't pay referral commissions.
- Don't publish fees, guarantees or "best" claims in ads or pages.
- Don't create one page per locality with the same text; city pages must stay distinct.
- Don't change the GBP address or category on the same day.

## 8. Enquiry flow: WhatsApp only, nothing stored on the website

This is now the only enquiry route.

- Every "Enquire" button opens WhatsApp with a message already typed: the service, the place and the page it came from. The visitor taps Send.
- No form, no name or phone field, and no lead stored in Supabase. The website no longer saves enquiries or click events.
- The lead endpoint returns "410 Gone", and the old form component is deleted from the code.
- The admin pages still show leads saved before this change; no new ones are added.

What this means for you: the enquiry lands in WhatsApp Business. Set up:
- Labels by service (Income tax, GST, Company, Audit, NRI, Foreign company) so each chat is sorted.
- Quick replies for the first response, and a reply time of under two working hours.
- An away message out of hours, with the Calendly link.

Measurement without storing leads: use WhatsApp Business' own reports, Google Business Profile calls and direction requests, and Search Console for search traffic.

## 10. Country plan: ranking in the US, UK, UAE, Singapore and other high-value markets

Scope rule: the firm advises on Indian law only. It can't give US, UK, UAE or Singapore tax advice; those need local licences (CPA, chartered accountant in the UK, local adviser). So the content and pages sell Indian-side services to people and companies in those countries.

How it ranks:
- Content in English that names the country in the question: "Set up a subsidiary in India from Singapore", "NRI in Dubai selling Chennai property", "India DTAA with the UK".
- One page per country for the main services (US, UK and UAE pages exist; Singapore is next).
- Google serves results by the searcher's country. English pages with clear local terms rank in those countries without needing other languages.
- Citations to official sources (Indian government portals, treaty texts) help AI engines cite the page.
- Listings and profiles: LinkedIn for the firm and CA Rushil; Indian listings for the firm; no paid listing directories abroad unless checked for ICAI compliance.
