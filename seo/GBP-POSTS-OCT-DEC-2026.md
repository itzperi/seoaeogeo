### Post 1 · week of 13 Oct · Income tax for HNIs (Besant Nagar)
**Link:** `https://www.csrushil.com/income-tax-consultant-besant-nagar-chennai?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=income-tax-consultant-besant-nagar-chennai`

> Moved back to Chennai after years abroad? Your foreign income may not be taxable in India straight away.
>
> Many returning NRIs qualify as "resident but not ordinarily resident" (RNOR) for a transition period. During that time, foreign income earned outside India and not linked to India generally stays outside Indian tax, and Schedule FA reporting isn't required. Status is worked out year by year from your past years of non-residence and the days you spent in India, so the travel record matters.
>
> Once you become ordinarily resident, worldwide income and foreign assets (bank accounts, RSUs, pensions, property) are taxable and must be reported every year.
>
> Families in Besant Nagar often bring returns that mix foreign pensions, RSUs, rental income and property or share sales. Our page for Besant Nagar residents explains how residency, foreign income and capital gains fit together. Meetings are at our Anna Nagar office or online.

### Post 2 · week of 20 Oct · GST litigation (GSTAT, Coimbatore)
**Link:** `https://www.csrushil.com/gst-litigation-coimbatore?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=gst-litigation-coimbatore`

> GST appeal against a Coimbatore order? The GST Appellate Tribunal now has a Tamil Nadu State Bench sitting in Coimbatore.
>
> A GST dispute moves up a fixed ladder:
> 1. Reply to the show-cause notice, usually within 30 days.
> 2. First appeal in Form APL-01 within three months of the order, with a pre-deposit of 10% of the disputed tax.
> 3. Second appeal to the GSTAT, with a further pre-deposit of 10%.
>
> Tamil Nadu's GSTAT benches are at Chennai, Coimbatore and Madurai. The GSTAT e-filing portal assigns the bench when you file, so check it there rather than assuming the nearest city.
>
> A practising Chartered Accountant is an authorised representative before GST officers, the Appellate Authority and the GSTAT under Section 116 of the CGST Act.
>
> Our Coimbatore page covers the issues manufacturers there face most often. Matters are handled online from our Anna Nagar, Chennai office.

### Post 3 · week of 27 Oct · Private limited company (Coimbatore)
**Link:** `https://www.csrushil.com/company-registration-coimbatore?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=company-registration-coimbatore`

> Registering a company in Coimbatore? It's done online on the MCA portal through SPICe+, and you don't need to visit any office.
>
> A company with its registered office in Coimbatore district comes under the Registrar of Companies, Coimbatore. A private limited company needs at least two directors and two shareholders (they can be the same people), and there's no minimum paid-up capital. At least one director must have stayed in India for 182 days or more in the financial year.
>
> Before filing, each director needs a Class 3 digital signature. PAN and Aadhaar details must match exactly, because a mismatch is one of the most common causes of delay.
>
> After incorporation: the first board meeting and auditor appointment within 30 days, share certificates within 60 days, and INC-20A within 180 days.
>
> Our Coimbatore page explains Pvt Ltd, LLP and OPC options for local businesses, handled remotely from Anna Nagar, Chennai.

### Post 4 · week of 3 Nov · Income tax for HNIs (Alwarpet)
**Link:** `https://www.csrushil.com/income-tax-consultant-alwarpet-chennai?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=income-tax-consultant-alwarpet-chennai`

> Doctors, advocates and consultants in Alwarpet: presumptive tax or full books?
>
> The presumptive scheme treats a fixed share of professional receipts as income. It's simple and suits a lean practice. A clinic or chamber with rent, staff, consumables and equipment often has higher real costs, and in that case maintaining full books can lower the tax. The right answer changes as the practice grows, so it's worth comparing both every year before filing.
>
> Other points that come up for professionals:
> - advance tax instalments, so interest doesn't build up
> - rental income from residential or commercial property
> - the old vs new regime comparison for the household
> - replying promptly when an AIS mismatch notice arrives
>
> Our Alwarpet page sets out how professional and rental income are handled. Meetings are at our Anna Nagar office or online.

### Post 5 · week of 10 Nov · GST litigation (Madurai)
**Link:** `https://www.csrushil.com/gst-litigation-madurai?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=gst-litigation-madurai`

> Received a GST show-cause notice in Madurai or southern Tamil Nadu? Note the deadline first.
>
> Most show-cause notices allow about 30 days to reply. A missed reply can lead to an order confirming the full demand, interest and penalty, which leaves only an appeal with a mandatory pre-deposit.
>
> For FY 2024-25 onwards, Section 74A is a single demand provision replacing Sections 73 and 74. Notices for FY 2023-24 and earlier still follow the older sections, so check which one your notice cites.
>
> If an order is passed, the first appeal in APL-01 is due within three months. The GST Appellate Tribunal lists Madurai among its three Tamil Nadu benches, and High Court matters from the area go to the Madurai Bench of the Madras High Court (writs need an advocate).
>
> Our Madurai page covers the full ladder. Replies and appeals are filed online from our Anna Nagar, Chennai office.

### Post 6 · week of 17 Nov · Private limited company (Chennai pillar)
**Link:** `https://www.csrushil.com/private-limited-company-incorporation-chennai?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=private-limited-company-incorporation-chennai`

> Pvt Ltd or LLP for a Chennai startup? It mostly comes down to funding.
>
> Choose a private limited company if you plan to raise equity, issue ESOPs or bring in investors, because an LLP can't issue shares. An LLP suits professional firms and closely held businesses that want lighter compliance: its accounts need an audit only above ₹40 lakh turnover or ₹25 lakh contribution. Both give limited liability.
>
> For a private limited company in Chennai, registration usually takes 7–12 working days once documents and digital signatures are ready, or about three weeks starting from scratch. The Registrar of Companies, Chennai covers Tamil Nadu except nine western districts, which ROC Coimbatore handles.
>
> Your home can be the registered office, with a utility bill not older than two months and, where needed, the owner's no-objection letter.
>
> Our page walks through documents, steps and first-year compliance.

### Post 7 · week of 24 Nov · Income tax for HNIs (Coimbatore promoter families)
**Link:** `https://www.csrushil.com/income-tax-consultant-coimbatore?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=income-tax-consultant-coimbatore`

> Coimbatore promoter families: is your tax position being looked at as a whole?
>
> The firm's or company's auditor focuses on the entity's books. A family's tax position spreads further: partners, directors, an HUF, rented factory land, farmland and investments.
>
> Some points worth reviewing every year:
> - A partner's share of profit is exempt, because the firm has already paid tax on it. Remuneration and interest on capital are taxable as business income in the partner's hands.
> - Salary vs dividend from a family company depends on each person's slab, the company's tax rate and whether the person actually works in the business.
> - Every return should match the AIS, so notices don't follow.
>

### Post 8 · week of 1 Dec · GST litigation (Tiruppur export refunds)
**Link:** `https://www.csrushil.com/gst-litigation-tiruppur?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=gst-litigation-tiruppur`

> Tiruppur exporters: GST refund held up or cut down? It can be contested.
>
> For knitwear and garment exporters, most GST disputes are about money stuck in refunds rather than tax demands:
> - IGST refunds on shipping bills held for data mismatches
> - refunds of unutilised credit on exports under a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) reduced or rejected
> - inverted duty refund claims cut down
>
> A refund rejection notice (RFD-08) is answered in RFD-09 with the documents and computations the officer has questioned. If the refund is still rejected, the order can be challenged by a first appeal in APL-01 and then before the GST Appellate Tribunal.
>
> Monthly GSTR-2B and shipping bill reconciliation makes most of these issues easier to answer.
>
> Our Tiruppur page sets out each route. Matters are handled online from our Anna Nagar, Chennai office.
