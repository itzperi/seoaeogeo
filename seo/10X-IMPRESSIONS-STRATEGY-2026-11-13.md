# 10x impressions strategy: csrushil.com (SEO + AEO + GEO + Google Business Profile)

**Prepared:** 13 Nov 2026.

**Baseline:** Search Console, last 3 months: **3,820 impressions across 131 pages**. 73 of the 201 sitemap pages had none.

**Goal:** 10x, which is about 38,000 impressions a quarter. The owner's stretch figure is 100,000.

> **Honest framing.** No one can promise Google numbers. A 10x rise is achievable in 6–12 months by copying what already works, at a steady publishing pace, with authority built in parallel. 100,000 a quarter (26x) needs that plus 12–18 months of compounding and real link growth. Track the milestones in section 9 and adjust.

### The winning pattern (copy this)
Every top guide answers a specific, regulation-named question that people across India (or across Tamil Nadu) ask, not just people in Chennai:

| Winner | Impressions | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| ROC Chennai vs ROC Coimbatore | 250 | A named authority + a "which one is mine" decision; statewide |
| Section 74A GST notice | 237 | A named new section + time limits + penalty; new law, few good pages |
| Company name rejection reasons | 223 | A painful moment, a named process (SPICe+ Part A), a fix list |
| Form 3CEB due date | 186 | A form number + a due date + applicability; searched in 5 countries |
| Government fees for company registration | 161 | A cost question answered with official figures (ICAI-safe) |
| TN stamp duty on incorporation | 85 | State-specific figure, rarely explained well |
| JDA tax for landowners | 84 | A specific transaction + worked example |
| SPICe+ step by step | 62 | A named form + "(2026)" in the title |
| E-way bill detention, Section 129 | 54 | A named section + an urgent problem (goods stuck) |

**Formula:** `[named form / section / authority / rule]` + `[deadline, cost, limit, penalty, "vs", rejection or step-by-step]` + a year where the facts change + India- or TN-wide scope.

**What wins on the page:**
- 1,600–2,000 words;
- a 40–60-word answer under the H1;
- question-led H2s;
- at least one table;
- 4–7 FAQs;
- links to official sources;
- links into the money page.

### What does not work
- **Generic topics with national competition** get nothing: "advance tax deadlines", "old vs new tax regime", "LLP vs OPC vs Pvt Ltd", "outgrown bookkeeping signs". 19 posts from 19 Sep 2026 are at zero.
- **Thin location variants** average 6 impressions. Google rarely ranks a 2-year-old site's area pages over directories.
- **Tools nobody links to** stay undiscovered.

## 3. Lever A: grow the top 30 pages ("85 → 850")

For each page, run the same 6 steps every 90 days:
1. **Find the queries.** In Search Console, open Pages → the page → Queries (3 months). Note the queries at position 5–30.
2. **Title and description:**
   - put the top query in the first 40 characters;
   - add the year where the facts are dated;
   - keep the title ≤60 characters and the description ≤155;
   - no "best", fees or guarantees.
3. **Missing sub-questions.** For each query the page doesn't answer, add a question H2 and a 40–60-word answer. Mine People Also Ask and related searches first.
4. **Freshness.** Re-check every figure and date, then set `updated` (blog) or let the page dates regenerate. AI tools favour content under 3 months old.
5. **Links:**
   - 3–5 internal links into the page from its spoke posts, with exact anchors;
   - 1 link out to the money page and 1 to the matching tool.
6. **Off-site.** One mention or link per quarter: a TaxGuru/CAclubIndia article, a LinkedIn post, a YouTube short.
