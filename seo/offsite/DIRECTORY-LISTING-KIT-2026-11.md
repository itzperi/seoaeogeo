## 4. Rules for every listing

- Never pay for placement, a "sponsored" slot, a badge or a "verified" upgrade.
- Never ask to be included in a "top firms" or "best CA" list.
- Leave fee, price and "starting from" fields blank.
- Use only real office photos. No stock images, no client documents.
- Do not copy reviews or ratings from one site to another, and do not show any rating on csrushil.com.
- Keep a list of every listing URL and the date it went live, and send it to the agent. Directories that allow it can then be linked from the site's organisation details.
