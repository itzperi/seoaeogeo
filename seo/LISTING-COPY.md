## Where to register (priority order)

1. **ICAI member/firm directory** — highest authority, since it's the
   regulator's own listing. Confirm/update via your ICAI member login.
2. **Google Business Profile** — see `GBP-CHECKLIST.md`, this is the most
   important one and isn't a simple "directory listing," it's covered
   separately.
3. **Sulekha** — sulekha.com — has dedicated categories for both
   "Chartered Accountants → Chennai" and "Chartered Accountants → Anna
   Nagar, Chennai." List under both, plus the GST Consultants category.
4. **Justdial** — justdial.com — search for your business first to check if
   an unclaimed/outdated listing already exists (common for established
   firms); claim and correct it rather than creating a duplicate.
5. **IndiaMART** — indiamart.com — B2B-oriented directory, list your
   services as offerings.
6. **RegisterKaro** and **QwikFilings** — service-marketplace directories
   for CA firms; lower authority but easy citation-volume wins.

## Before you submit anywhere

Double-check every listing against the NAP block above **before** hitting
submit — a single character difference (e.g. "Rd" vs "Road", or a different
phone format) across platforms is exactly the kind of inconsistency that
hurt the original site's local ranking, per the original audit. When in
doubt, copy-paste rather than retype.
